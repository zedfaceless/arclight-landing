The silent leak

Revenue doesn't disappear loudly.
It leaks one renewal at a time.

Every subscription business runs a gap between what the contract says and what actually lands in the bank. It never appears as a line item on the P&L — so nobody owns it, and it compounds quarter after quarter.

01 / PAYMENTS

Failed payments & dunning gaps

Involuntary churn is the cheapest churn to fix and the easiest to miss. An expired card or a soft decline can quietly cancel a $40k account while the customer still thinks they're paying you.

Industry benchmark: up to 9% of subscription revenue
02 / CHURN

Silent churn & usage decay

Accounts rarely cancel on a Tuesday. They stop inviting seats, skip two quarters of onboarding, and go quiet in the support channel — then leave at renewal and tell you it was a budget decision.

Churn signal visible 60+ days earlier in product data
03 / PRICING

Discount & seat drift

A custom discount gets approved to close one deal. Eighteen months later it's permanent, the seat count is 40% above usage, and the original justification has left the company.

Roughly 1 in 6 enterprise discounts is unapproved or expired
04 / DATA

Billing & CRM mismatch

Your CRM reports $4.2M ARR. Your billing system collected $3.9M. Finance rebuilds the bridge in a spreadsheet every month, and nobody trusts the board deck by the time it ships.

Average variance: 3.1% between CRM ARR and cash collected
